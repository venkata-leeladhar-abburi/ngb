## 8. Homepage: sections, reasons and copy

The homepage tells one story in 12 beats: **I did it → here's proof → here's free help → people like you did it → pick your plan.** English is the base; Telugu lines are for the `తె` version and for supporting lines in the English version.

Copy rules applied: the hero has at most 4 text elements; one buying label ("Start my plan") and one free label ("Try free tools") everywhere; no invented numbers; quotes stay under 3 lines; no two sections share a layout.

### 0. Loader

**Why it's here:** sets the cinematic tone while the hero image loads (under 1.2 s). Skipped on repeat visits and with reduced motion.

**Layout:** black; diagonal gloss stripes sweep once; the NGB mark snaps in red. No copy.
