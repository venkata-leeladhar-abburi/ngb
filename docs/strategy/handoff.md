### Digital behaviour that shapes the build

- **Instagram in-app browser is the main door.** Most visits start from a bio link, story link or Reel. Pages must load fast on 4G, work without login, and survive the in-app browser's payment redirects.
- **Android, mid-range phones.** Design at 360–412 px wide first. Keep the hero image under 150 KB.
- **They read Tenglish.** Young Telugu users chat in Telugu written in English letters. Use it for hooks and buttons' supporting lines; keep fitness terms (protein, reps, sets) in English.
- **WhatsApp is trust.** A WhatsApp button (with a real human replying) converts hesitant buyers better than a contact form.
- **UPI and low round-number prices.** ₹999 / ₹1,499 / ₹2,999 read better than ₹1,853. Show the UPI logo at checkout.

## 4. Website strategy

The site is a **give-first sales machine**: free tools and videos earn trust, every free thing ends by recommending exactly one program, and the programs are priced so a student can say yes. The north-star metric is paid program sales per month.

### Goals and KPIs

| Level | Metric | How it's measured |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North star | Paid programs sold per month | Razorpay orders, net of refunds |
| Revenue | Average order value (AOV) and membership MRR | Orders + active subscriptions |
| Engagement | % of visitors who use a free tool or play a video | GA4 events `tool_complete`, `video_play` |
| Lead capture | % of tool users who leave a WhatsApp number or email | `lead_submit` / `tool_complete` |
| Conversion | Lead → purchase within 14 days; visit → purchase | Cohort report |
| Checkout | Checkout start → payment success | `checkout_start` / `purchase` |
| Quality | Refund rate; week-4 program completion; review score | Razorpay + program app data |

Targets are set after a 30-day baseline. Do not publish or pitch invented conversion benchmarks.

### Offer ladder

Every visitor should see a next step that fits their trust level and budget. Prices below are recommendations anchored on the market data in section 2. `[CONFIRM]` all prices with Nawin.

| Step | Offer | Price (recommendation) | Job it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 · Free | Tools, 3 free videos per muscle group, weekly Telugu tips on WhatsApp | ₹0 | Earn trust, collect the lead |
| 1 · Free lead magnet | **7-Day Starter Plan** (workouts + Telugu meal plan) sent on WhatsApp after a tool result | ₹0 for a WhatsApp number | Start the habit; prove the coaching works |
| 2 · Core programs | **Foundation Plan** (beginners, 8 weeks) · **Shred 12** (fat loss) · **Mass Builder** (weight gain, with hostel version) · **Master the Bodyweight** (home calisthenics) | ₹1,499–₹2,999 one-time, lifetime access | Main revenue; clear outcome per goal |
| 3 · Membership | **NGB Evolve Club**: full video library, a new plan every month, community, monthly live Q&A with Nawin | ₹499/month or ₹3,999/year | Recurring revenue; keeps finishers inside |
| 4 · Premium | **1:1 Coaching** with Team NGB, weekly check-ins, plan changes, one monthly call with Nawin; limited seats | ₹9,999 per 12 weeks; NRI tier in USD | High margin; aspirational anchor for the rest |

The current site sells Weekly / Monthly / Yearly access. Drop the weekly plan: a week is too short to see results and it makes refunds likely.

### Pricing psychology

- **Show the daily price.** "₹1,999 for 12 weeks = ₹24 a day. Less than your evening chai and samosa."
- **Anchor against a trainer.** A local personal trainer costs ₹8,000–12,000 a month; one line comparing the two makes ₹1,999 feel easy.
- **Three cards, middle one featured.** Shred 12 or Mass Builder (whichever the Insights poll says is the #1 goal) is "Most popular".
- **Round rupee prices** (₹999, ₹1,499, ₹1,999). Never ₹1,853.
- **Real urgency only.** Programs start in monthly batches ("Batch 07 starts 1 October"). Batches create a community and an honest deadline. No fake countdown timers.
- **Risk reversal beside the button.** "7-day refund, no questions" `[CONFIRM]` plus GST invoice and Razorpay logo.
- **Bundles.** Program + 3 months of Club at checkout as a one-tap add-on.

### Conversion architecture

1. **One CTA label for buying, everywhere:** "Start my plan". One label for the free path: "Try free tools". No "Join now", "Explore programs", "Get started" variants.
2. **Every free thing ends in one recommendation.** A calorie result for fat loss recommends Shred 12; a weight-gain result recommends Mass Builder; a skinny beginner gets Foundation Plan.
3. **"Find my plan" quiz** (4 questions: goal, where you train, food, level) for anyone who can't choose.
4. **Proof sits next to the price.** A transformation that matches the program is placed directly above its buy button.
5. **Checkout in 2 steps:** phone number + OTP, then UPI. No account creation before payment.
6. **Sticky mobile bar** after the tools section: program name, price, "Start my plan".
7. **WhatsApp for the unsure.** "Doubts unnaya? Talk to Team NGB" on every program page.

### After the sale (retention is part of the site)

- Welcome page with the first workout, a WhatsApp group link and a 30-second voice note from Nawin.
- Weekly check-in form (weight, photo, one question) that feeds the transformations gallery, with consent.
- Week-10 prompt to join the Club so finishers don't drop off.

## 5. Attraction strategy

Instagram creates the attention; the site turns it into sales. The engine is a loop: a Reel sends people to a free tool, the tool result gets shared and captured on WhatsApp, the starter plan leads to a program, and every finished program becomes the next Reel.

&#91;embedded content: growth loop · 6 steps\]

The loop only compounds if results are shareable (story cards) and transformations are collected with consent from day one.

### Instagram: the front door

- **Bio link goes to `/start`, not the homepage.** A short page: one line, three big buttons (Find my plan · Free tools · Programs). The homepage is for people who come from Google or YouTube.
- **"Comment PLAN" automation.** Reels end with "Comment PLAN, I'll DM you the free calculator." An Instagram-approved DM automation tool sends the tool link. This beats "link in bio" by removing two taps.
- **Story link stickers** point to one tool each, never to a generic page.
- **Three pinned posts:** the journey Reel, the free tools Reel, the latest transformation.
- **Reel series built around site tools:** *Hostel diet in ₹100 a day* → protein tool; *Andhra meals, how much protein?* → Telugu Plate counter; *Skinny to strong in hostel* → Mass Builder; *No gym, no problem* → Master the Bodyweight.

### Free tools as lead magnets

| --- | --- | --- |
| Calorie calculator | "Fat loss ki exact ga entha tinali?" `[TE REVIEW]` | Shred 12 |
| Weight-gain calculator | "Skinny ga unnava? Nee number idi." `[TE REVIEW]` | Mass Builder |

The **Telugu Plate** counter is the one tool no national competitor has. It turns the #1 local problem (low protein, rice-heavy meals) into a shareable, funny and useful moment.

### Share cards

Every tool result renders a 1080 × 1920 story card in the brand style ("My daily target: 2,480 kcal · Built with NGB Evolve") with one-tap share to Instagram Stories. Each share is a free ad to the sharer's friends, who are the same audience.

### WhatsApp: where trust turns into purchase

| 2 | Telugu food tip: 3 cheap protein swaps for rice-heavy meals |
| 3 | One client transformation that matches their goal |

### YouTube, search and community

- **YouTube:** every video links to one tool or program in the first line of the description and a pinned comment.
- **Search (Telugu + English):** articles and video pages for the questions people type: *weight gain diet in Telugu*, *hostel diet plan*, *fat loss tips in Telugu*, *home workout Telugu*. Each ends with the matching tool.
- **Community:** a free WhatsApp channel for tips; paid members join their batch's group. Monthly challenge (e.g. "30 days, 10,000 steps") with the leaderboard on the site.

## 10. Voice, microcopy and bilingual rules

Write like Nawin talks to a younger brother at the gym: short, direct, specific, and never fake. If a line would sound odd coming out of his mouth in a Reel, it doesn't go on the site.

### Four voice rules

1. **Anna, not guru.** First person from Nawin ("I", "my plan"), second person to the reader ("you"). No "we at NGB".
2. **Short.** Headlines up to 8 words; hero headlines in Rush Driver up to 5. Sentences under 15 words where possible.
3. **Specific.** Kilos, weeks, rupees, grams, real foods. "Eat 3 eggs more" beats "boost your protein".
4. **Honest.** No guaranteed results, no 30-day miracles, no fake scarcity, no invented numbers.

### Words to use and avoid

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| plan, batch, day one, real food, Telugu food | journey to greatness, unlock your potential, transform your life |
| Start my plan (the one buy label) | Join now, Get started, Enroll, Explore programs |
| skinny, belly, strong, lean (words they use) | fat guy, weak, ugly, any body-shaming |
| 12 weeks, 4 days a week | fast, instant, guaranteed, miracle, hack |
| hostel, mess, pappu, perugu, rice | clean eating, macros-only jargon without explaining it |
| Team NGB (for the support team) | experts, gurus |

### Formats

- **Rupees:** ₹1,999; Indian grouping for large numbers (₹1,00,000). Always show ₹ per day next to a program price.
- **Units:** kg, cm, kcal, g, L. Offer ft + in for height input only.
- **Time:** "12 weeks", never "3 months". Dates as "1 October".
- **Casing:** Rush Driver headlines are written in capitals in the design, but typed in sentence case in the code and uppercased with CSS so screen readers read words, not letters.
- **Buttons:** verb first, 1–3 words: Start my plan · Try free tools · Show my number · Send my plan · Count my plate.

### Bilingual rules

1. **Three registers, each with its job.** English is the default site. Telugu script is the full `తె` version. Tenglish (Telugu in English letters) appears only as short supporting hooks in the English version, never for prices, legal text or instructions.
2. **Transcreate, don't translate.** A native Telugu copywriter adapts each line to sound natural. Machine translation is not allowed on the live site.
3. **Fitness words stay in English inside Telugu:** protein, sets, reps, kcal, cardio, plan. That is how the audience speaks.
4. **Neutral Telugu in the UI.** Keep region-only slang ("mawa" in Telangana, "bava" in coastal Andhra) for Reels, not the website.
5. **Telugu needs its own fonts.** Rush Driver has no Telugu letters (checked in the font file). Use **Anek Telugu** (ExtraBold, condensed width, skewed −8° to match the brand lean) for Telugu headlines and **Noto Sans Telugu** (400–600) for Telugu body text. Both are on Google Fonts.
6. **Give Telugu room.** Telugu text runs taller and 20–30% longer. Body line-height 1.7; test every headline at 360 px.
7. **One script per headline**, except English loanwords ("నీ Day One ఈరోజే").

### Microcopy library

| Moment | Copy |
| --- | --- |
| Required field empty | Please enter your weight. |
| Number out of range | Enter a weight between 30 and 200 kg. |
| Invalid phone | Enter a 10-digit mobile number. |
| Loading a result | Calculating your number… |
| Lead saved | Done. Check WhatsApp in a minute. |
| WhatsApp consent | I agree to get my plan and fitness tips from NGB Evolve on WhatsApp. I can stop anytime. |
| Copy link | Link copied. |
| Video locked | This workout is inside Mass Builder. |
| Offline | You're offline. We'll save your check-in and send it when you're back. |

| Cookie banner | We use cookies to measure what helps. Accept · Only essential |

### Claims and compliance

- Follow ASCI (Advertising Standards Council of India) rules for health claims and influencer disclosures: no guaranteed outcomes, and label any paid brand collaboration.
- Every before/after has written consent, is unedited, and shows weeks and starting point.
- Health line on every program and tool page: *This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor if you have a medical condition.*
- Supplements are never required to buy or finish a program.

## 11. Design and motion direction

The ChatGPT mockups nailed the mood (red studio, Rush Driver, cut corners) but read as a template: every section is a row of cards, CTAs repeat with five different labels, and the people and numbers are invented. The build keeps the mood and fixes the structure, following the Taste skill and UI/UX Pro Max rules below. Tokens, fonts and components live in the [NGB Evolve design system](https://claude.ai/artifact/CNfXfKiXyRRcGphi9AQqpU) (private until shared).

### Design read

**Reading this as:** a premium-consumer creator site with a light product layer (tools, checkout) for Telugu youth on Android, in a cinematic sports-campaign language, built with native CSS / Tailwind v4, GSAP ScrollTrigger for two pinned moments and Motion for micro-interactions.

| Taste dial | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Design variance | 8 | Poster type, subject in front of type, forward lean; not a symmetric template |
| Motion intensity | 7 desktop / 5 phone | Cinematic, but mid-range Android phones on 4G must stay smooth |
| Visual density | 4 | Big type and photos, few words per section |

UI/UX Pro Max's product match for fitness is "Vibrant & Block-based + Dark Mode (OLED)" with motion-driven secondary styling; for creator link-in-bio pages it recommends "Conversion-Optimized + Social Proof". Both fit this brief.

### Review of the ChatGPT mockups

|  | Journey and hero use AI renders of Nawin; replace with real photos and a real shoot |

### Rules the build must follow

- **Theme lock:** the page is dark. Red studio sections (hero, programs) are a deliberate colour-block device used twice, not random section flips.
- **Accent lock:** studio red is the only UI accent. The ember gradient lives only in photo lighting; gold appears once, on "Most popular".
- **Shape lock:** hard edges with the chamfer cut on buttons and tool cards; cards at 4 px; circles only for play buttons.
- **Eyebrows:** at most one per three sections (the hero's `// NAWIN GOLDEN BOY` counts). No numbered section labels.
- **Separators:** no more than one middle dot per line in the live UI; the dots in this document are for reading, not for the build.
- **Dashes:** no em dashes or en dashes in any on-site copy (hyphens only).
- **Icons:** one library, Phosphor at 1.5 px stroke (Barbell, Heartbeat, Timer, Fire, Drop, ForkKnife). This replaces the Lucide note in the design system. No hand-drawn icons.
- **Motion:** every animation has one reason (story, hierarchy, feedback). No custom cursor. Everything has a `prefers-reduced-motion` version.
- **Heights:** `min-height: 100dvh` for full-screen sections, never `100vh` (mobile address bars).

### Photography and video (the real differentiator)

No AI-generated people anywhere on the live site. Commission one studio day and collect archives:

- [ ] Hero: Nawin on a red seamless backdrop, hard top-left key light, 3 poses (front, three-quarter, back), cut-out ready
- [ ] One shot per program (4) and per goal tile (4), same lighting
- [ ] Journey: 5 real archive photos from 2022 to today with dates `[CONFIRM]`
- [ ] Food: overhead shots of a Telugu thali, a hostel mess plate, a high-protein breakfast (idli + eggs + milk)
- [ ] Video: 30 s journey clip for the hero play button; 3 free workout videos per muscle group; one 20 s voice note
- [ ] Transformations: before/after pairs from real members, with signed consent, unedited

### Lessons from award-winning sites

- **Fitonist** ([Awwwards Site of the Day, 25 Aug 2024](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/fitonist)) scored highest on animation and performance (7.60) and lowest on accessibility (7.00). Its lesson: a two-colour palette, a preloader and scroll-triggered motion win juries; skipping accessibility costs points. NGB should match the motion and beat it on accessibility.
- **One big idea, executed fully.** Award winners commit to one visual idea; here it is the subject standing in front of giant jagged type, repeated in hero, programs and final call.
- **Type is the design.** Rush Driver at poster size does the work that decoration does on average sites; keep everything around it quiet.
- **Real content beats effects.** Juries and buyers both reward real photography and real stories over generic 3D or stock.

## 12. UX, accessibility, performance, SEO and tracking

The site must feel instant inside Instagram's in-app browser on a mid-range Android phone on 4G. That single constraint drives most technical choices below.

### Recommended stack `[CONFIRM]` with the agency

| Layer | Recommendation | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front end | Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind v4, Motion for micro-interactions, GSAP ScrollTrigger for the 2 pinned sections | Fast static pages, fine motion control |
| Content | Headless CMS (e.g. Sanity) with English + Telugu fields for every page, program, tool and transformation | Team NGB edits copy and adds results without developers |
| Payments | Razorpay (UPI intent, QR, cards, netbanking, subscriptions for Club); international cards for NRI pricing | UPI is 83.4% of payment volume |
| Login | Phone number + OTP (e.g. MSG91 or Firebase phone auth) | No passwords; matches WhatsApp habits |
| WhatsApp | WhatsApp Business API through an approved provider (e.g. Interakt, AiSensy, Gupshup) | Starter plan delivery, nurture, payment reminders |
| Video | Adaptive streaming (e.g. Mux or Bunny Stream) with signed URLs for paid videos; low-bitrate renditions | Plays on 4G; paid videos can't be shared freely |
| Member area | Build on the same site, or use a creator course platform (e.g. Graphy, Exly) for v1 | A platform is faster to launch; custom is better long-term |

### Performance budget

| Metric | Budget |
| --- | --- |
| Largest Contentful Paint | under 2.5 s on a mid-range Android, 4G |
| Interaction to Next Paint | under 200 ms |
| Cumulative Layout Shift | under 0.1 |
| JavaScript on landing pages | under 200 KB compressed |
| Hero image | under 150 KB (AVIF/WebP, responsive sizes) |
| Fonts | Rush Driver subset to Latin caps + numerals + ₹, preloaded; Telugu fonts load only on `/te` pages |

The loader must never delay the hero image; it plays only while the hero is still loading.

### Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)

- Text contrast 4.5:1 (3:1 for 24 px+); `bone-muted` never on red.
- Visible focus ring on every control (`focus-ring` token).
- Labels above every input; `inputmode="numeric"` for weight, height, age, phone; errors below the field.
- Tap targets at least 44 × 44 px.
- Every video captioned in Telugu and English; transformations have descriptive alt text.
- `lang="te"` on Telugu pages; giant decorative words hidden from screen readers.
- All motion has a reduced-motion version; pinned sections become normal scroll.

### SEO

- **Own the name.** The homepage title targets "Nawin Golden Boy" and "NGB Evolve" so the official site outranks troll videos for his name. Add `Person` and `Organization` schema, linking to his main Instagram and YouTube with `sameAs`.
- **Page titles (examples):** Home: *NGB Evolve by Nawin Golden Boy | Telugu fitness programs* · Calorie tool: *Calorie Calculator (Telugu + English) | NGB Evolve* · Mass Builder: *Weight Gain Program for Hostel Students | NGB Evolve*
- **Schema:** `Product` + `Offer` for programs, `FAQPage` for FAQ, `VideoObject` for free workouts, `WebApplication` for tools.
- **Languages:** `hreflang` en-IN and te-IN on every page pair; a Telugu sitemap.
- **Content clusters:** weight gain, fat loss, hostel diet, home workouts, protein on Telugu food; each cluster ends in its tool and program.
- **Share images:** auto-generated Open Graph image per tool result and per program.

### Tracking

| Event | Fires when |
| --- | --- |
| `tool_start`, `tool_complete` | A tool is opened / a result is shown (with tool name) |
| `share_card` | A result card is shared |

| `quiz_complete` | Quiz result shown (with program) |
| `video_play`, `video_locked_click` | Free video plays / locked video tapped |

- Privacy policy, terms, refund policy, shipping/delivery policy (Razorpay requires these), contact page with business address and GSTIN.
